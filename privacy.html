Privacy policy

Last updated 7 November 2026

This policy explains what information Michiya Studio handles when you use our casual games and productivity apps, including Cat Café Sort, and when you use the website michiyastudio.in. Please read it with the terms of service.

If this policy changes in a material way, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Who we are

Michiya Studio makes the apps and this website. For questions about your information, write to keerthigowdasuguna@gmail.com. That address is also the contact for a privacy complaint.

We do not run player accounts. Cat Café Sort does not ask you to register, and it does not upload your save to a Michiya Studio server.

Where this policy applies

It applies to people who install our apps or visit this website, wherever they are. Local law can give you extra rights. Where a local law requires something this policy does not describe, we follow that law for people it covers. People in India, the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and certain US states have additional rights, described below under “Your rights and options.”

Information we handle

Information you send us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write: a question, a bug report, or a request about your information. We use that only to reply and to keep a record of the request.

Information stored on your device

Cat Café Sort saves progress on the phone or tablet: level, coins, lives, settings, and which kitties you have met. That save stays on the device. Clearing the app’s data, or deleting the app, removes it. We do not receive a copy.

Information collected for advertising

The free version of Cat Café Sort shows ads through AppLovin MAX. AppLovin asks advertising partners to bid. Partners that may be connected include Google (AdMob), Unity Ads, Meta, Smaato, Mintegral, Liftoff, InMobi, and Pangle. Those companies, and AppLovin, may receive:

  • a device advertising identifier
  • IP address and a coarse location derived from it (city or region, not a street address from GPS)
  • device type, operating system, language, and time zone
  • the app version and whether an ad was requested, shown, clicked, or rewarded
  • a coarse signal about the ad placement, such as a rewarded video or a banner

We do not send them your name, your email, your contacts, your photos, or the contents of your game save.

Information from the app stores

If you buy something, such as Remove Ads, Apple or Google processes the payment. We may receive a record that the purchase succeeded, a product identifier, and a transaction identifier so the app can unlock the feature. We do not receive your full card number.

What we do not collect in the game

Cat Café Sort does not ask for an account, a player name, a profile photo, a phone number, or access to your contacts, camera, microphone, or precise GPS. There is no player chat. The website does not have a sign-in and does not set its own analytics cookies.

Why we use information

  • To run the app. The on-device save lets you continue a level, hear sound, and keep settings. The basis is providing the app you chose to install.
  • To show and measure ads. Advertising identifiers and the related device data are used to request an ad, to limit how often the same ad appears, and to count whether it was shown. Personalized ads are requested only where you have allowed them, or where the law does not require a separate choice.
  • To complete a purchase. A store receipt is used to turn on the feature you bought.
  • To answer you. Email is used to reply to support and privacy requests.
  • To meet a legal duty. We may use or disclose information if a law, a court, or a competent authority requires it, or to respond to a claim about the apps or this site.

We do not sell your name or email. We do not ask for those in Cat Café Sort. Advertising partners may use an advertising identifier for their own ads under their policies, which is a separate disclosure from a sale of a contact list.

Who can see information

Advertising partners. AppLovin and the networks listed above receive the advertising data described above, so they can fill, measure, and limit ads. Their own privacy notices govern what they do after they receive it. Google’s partner information is at policies.google.com/privacy. AppLovin’s notice is at applovin.com/privacy.

App stores. Apple and Google see purchase and install information because you downloaded the app from them. Their rules govern that information.

Email. Messages you send to keerthigowdasuguna@gmail.com are stored in that mailbox, which is provided by Google.

Authorities. We may disclose information if we believe in good faith that the law requires it, or that it is needed to protect a person from serious harm or to respond to a legal claim.

We do not have a parent company, a group of affiliates, or other players who can see your save. There is no social profile to share.

International transfers

Michiya Studio is operated from India. Advertising companies and the email provider process information in other countries, including the United States and countries where their servers run. Those countries may not have the same data-protection law as the country where you live. Where the law requires a transfer safeguard, we rely on the mechanism the advertising partner provides for that transfer, such as a contract clause they offer to publishers. You can ask us which partners are active in the build you installed by writing to the contact address above.

Your rights and options

Ads on your device

  • iPhone and iPad. The game may ask for permission to track you across apps and websites owned by other companies. You can refuse. The game still runs. Ads can still appear, but they are less tied to activity in other apps. Change this later in Settings, under Privacy & Security, then Tracking.
  • Android. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings, under Privacy, then Ads. You can also opt out of ads personalization in that same place. The menu names vary slightly by Android version.

If you are in a region that requires a consent choice, the game shows a consent form before personalized ads are requested. You can withdraw that consent by contacting us. Where the form is offered again, you can also change the choice there.

Buying “Remove Ads,” when that purchase is available, turns off the banner and the ads between levels. Rewarded videos stay available because you choose to watch them.

Rights you can ask us to honour

Depending on where you live, you can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal information we hold about you, and for a copy of it
  • correct information that is wrong
  • delete information we hold
  • stop a use that the law lets you object to
  • withdraw a consent you previously gave, without affecting a use that already happened
  • receive information you gave us in a common file format, where that right applies and where we actually hold it

Write to keerthigowdasuguna@gmail.com with the request and the country you live in. We may ask for enough detail to match the request to an email thread. We do not hold the on-device save, so we cannot export or delete that copy; you delete it by removing the app’s data or the app itself. Advertising partners hold their own records. We will tell them to stop using information we sent, where we have a control to do that, and we will point you to their opt-out where we do not.

People in India can also use the rights in the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, including access, correction, erasure, and a grievance to us first. People in the EEA or the UK can complain to their local data-protection authority. People in California and other US states with a privacy statute can write to us to ask what we collected and to ask for deletion; we will handle the request as that statute requires. We do not run a separate California, Canada, or India microsite, because this page is the notice for every region.

Cookies and similar technologies

This website is a set of static pages. It does not set analytics cookies and it does not embed third-party advertising tags.

Inside the apps, advertising software uses device identifiers and similar storage the way a website uses a cookie: to remember an ad request, to cap how often an ad is shown, and, where allowed, to make the next ad more relevant. You can reset or limit that identifier with the iOS and Android controls above. Partners operate under their own policies once they receive the identifier.

How information is protected

The game save is kept in the app’s private storage on your device, which the operating system separates from other apps. We do not operate a player database for Cat Café Sort. Email we receive is kept in the mailbox used for support. No method of storage is perfect. If you write to us, avoid sending passwords, payment card numbers, or government identity numbers; we do not need them.

How long information is kept

The save file stays on your device until you delete it. Email is kept for as long as needed to handle the request and for a reasonable period afterward in case you follow up, unless you ask us to delete it and the law does not require us to keep it. Advertising partners set their own retention periods. Purchase records from a store follow that store’s retention.

Children

The apps are not directed at children under 13, or under the higher age your country uses for a child’s consent. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, write to us and we will delete it.

Changes

The date at the top of this page is the date of the current version. Using the apps or the website after that date means the updated policy applies to new use.
